How we work out typical prices

Each range is the total you'd typically pay at a UK independent garage: parts, labour and VAT.

We build the ranges from published garage price guides, booking-platform averages and fixed-price menus, using several sources for each repair where we can.

For now, each repair has one national range for all cars. Prices for your make and model, and main dealer prices, can be higher. Where we have limited data, we tell you on the result.

Kerbside provides benchmark data for transparency purposes only. Results are not a professional assessment and do not imply that any garage has acted improperly. Prices vary by region, vehicle condition, and supplier. Always obtain multiple quotes.
